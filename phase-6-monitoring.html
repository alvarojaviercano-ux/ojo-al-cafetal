Phase 6 of 6 · CRISP-ML

Monitoring and Maintenance

How the app should be watched and improved over time. This phase is a plan.

Status: the app is live, but long-term monitoring has not started. This page explains what I would do next, and why.

Continuous supervision

Keep a record of each photo the app receives (only if the user agrees): the answer, the confidence, and whether the user says the answer was right. Review the share of low-confidence answers every month.

Degradation detection

The model was trained with robusta photos from one place. It can get worse with other cameras, other light, other varieties, or other diseases. A rise in low-confidence answers or in “not a suitable photo” warnings would be a first sign.

Model updating

  • Add my own photos of Colombian arabica leaves (Castillo and Caturra) and test the model with them.
  • Add photos of other diseases (for example Cercospora and Phoma) so they stop showing up as false positives.
  • Train again on the computer with the new photos, check the results by plant, and publish the new model files.

Risk review

Review with a coffee technician whether the advice is safe. Check again if the Q-Learning simulation still matches the guidance of Cenicafé, and replace my assumed costs and rewards with real ones when I have them.
