Telling the diseases apart is much harder with these features. That is why the app does not name the disease; it says if the leaf looks sick. Precision and recall per class are still to be added.

Cross-validation

  • By plant. Every score above that says “grouped by plant” keeps the 4 photos of a plant together. It is a fair test, because the model never sees a plant it will be tested on.
  • Same outline as the app. The first models used the expert’s outline. The app cuts the leaf by itself, and this gave a different result. I trained again with the automatic outline, so the number matches what the user gets.

K-Means: where is the damage?

K-Means groups the pixels of the leaf by color. A group counts as damage if most of its pixels are yellow, orange or brown. The app paints these pixels in magenta and gives the percent of the leaf.

Rust level in RoCoLeTypical K-Means result
Level 2 (6–20% by the expert scale)about 4.5%
Level 3 (21–50%)about 7.8%
Level 4 (more than 50%)about 17.1%

The order is right, but K-Means gives lower numbers than the expert scale. It also misses rust level 1. So the app calls it an estimate of the zone with signs of damage, not a measure of damage.

Q-Learning: what to do?

I made a simulation of one coffee plot. The agent can keep watching, improve crop management, or apply fungicide. The rewards follow Cenicafé’s guidance: resistant varieties (about 80% of the planted area) do not need chemical control, and susceptible ones like Caturra should be treated when the rust level grows.

Honest note: the simulation numbers (costs and rewards) are my assumptions. The advice is a help, not a diagnosis, and a technician should confirm it.

Risk analysis

RISK

Other diseases

The model only knows rust and red spider mite. A leaf with white mold or other spots can still be called “sick”.

RISK

Messy photos

If there are several leaves in the photo, the automatic cut can include more than one. The app asks for one leaf on a plain background and warns when the cut looks wrong.

RISK

Other coffee types

The training photos are robusta. In a few tests with other photos the answers were right, but the confidence was only 60–70%. A real test with arabica is still needed.

RISK

Rust level 1

Very early rust is the hardest case: the spots are small, and K-Means often finds almost nothing.

RISK

Class imbalance

There are many more healthy leaves than sick ones, so I report accuracy together with the grouped test.

Expert review

The advice in the app follows the guidance of Cenicafé (resistant and susceptible varieties). A coffee technician has not yet reviewed the results. Until then, the app tells the user to check more leaves and talk to a technician before applying any product.
