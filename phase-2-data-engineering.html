Phase 2 of 6 · CRISP-ML

Data Engineering

From photos to numbers a model can use.

Selection

I used RoCoLe as the base: 1,560 photos with an expert label and the outline of each leaf. A second dataset of arabica leaves (JMuBEN) is a way to test later, after I check its license.

Cleaning

  • Leaf outline. For training I used the expert’s outline of each leaf (1,560 of 1,560 outlines were read with no errors).
  • Automatic outline in the app. The app has no expert, so it cuts the leaf by itself with GrabCut. This gave a different result from the expert’s outline, so I trained again with the automatic one (see Phase 4).
  • Same photo, same result. GrabCut has a random part. I fixed its seed, so the same photo always gives the same answer.

Transformation

From each photo I took 26 numbers, only from the leaf pixels.

COLOR

Color numbers

Mean and spread of the red, green and blue channels, and of hue, saturation and value. Share of yellow, orange and brown. The three main colors (K-Means with 3 groups).

SPOTS

Spot numbers

Number of spots, mean and largest spot size, share of the leaf with spots, share of brown, and texture.

SIZE

Leaf size

The share of the photo that the leaf covers.

Other steps

  • Classes. The 6 classes were grouped into healthy and sick for the main model. Rust levels were kept for the regression and for K-Means checks.
  • Plants. RoCoLe has 4 photos of each plant. I read the plant name from the file name so photos of one plant stay together.
  • Scaling. Logistic Regression and KNN use standardized numbers (StandardScaler).
