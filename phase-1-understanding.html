Business and Data Understanding

Why the project exists, what it must answer, and what data I have.

Business objectives

When a coffee plant gets sick, you see it on the leaf first. But we often find the disease too late, and the farmer loses coffee.

Goal: build a web app that finds coffee leaf diseases from a photo, so farmers can act on time.
  • It only needs a phone and the internet. No expert visit is needed for a first check.
  • It helps the farmer act on time and lose less coffee.

Key questions

Each question needs a different model from our course.

Question the model must answerModel
Is the leaf healthy or sick?Logistic Regression, KNN, Random Forest
Which disease is it?KNN
Which part of the leaf is damaged?K-Means
How much is damaged?Linear Regression
What is the best action?Q-Learning

There are about 26 times more healthy leaves than rust level 4 leaves. A model could learn to always say “healthy”. I fixed this by grouping the classes into healthy and sick for the main model.

Rust level, by how much of the leaf has spots: 1 = 1–5%, 2 = 6–20%, 3 = 21–50%, 4 = more than 50%.

Feasibility: the good and the bad

GOOD
  • An expert checked the labels
  • Photos taken with a phone, just like real use
  • It has rust levels (how bad the rust is)
  • Open license: CC BY 4.0
  • I already use Flask, scikit-learn and Render, so I need no new tools
LIMITS
  • It is robusta coffee, but Colombia grows mostly arabica
  • The photos are not from Colombia
  • The classes are not balanced
  • I only tested it with a few photos from other sources, not a full field study
  • I must give credit to the authors, and say if I change the data
Next step: take my own photos of Colombian arabica leaves (for example Castillo and Caturra) to test the model in real conditions.

License of RoCoLe: CC BY 4.0. I can use and change the data if I give credit to the authors, link to the license, and say what I changed. This does not mean the authors support this project. Dataset: Parraga-Alava et al. (2019), Mendeley Data, doi 10.17632/c5yvn32dzg.2.
